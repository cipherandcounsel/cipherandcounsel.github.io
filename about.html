About · Mission & Purpose

Here to make a direct, positive impact.

I am a Cybersecurity student in the UTSA College of AI, Cyber, and Computing Honors College and the founder of Cipher & Counsel. My mission is to make a direct, positive impact on privacy, AI governance, and digital responsibility. I am passionate about protecting critical infrastructure and dedicated to protecting organizations against the most advanced attackers in the world.

Security failures rarely end with a technical fix. After an incident, the decisive questions concern accountability, legal duty, public communication, and cost. Cybersecurity gives me the technical judgment to understand how systems fail. Governance, risk, and compliance work lets me decide how institutions should respond and translate that response into controls people can adopt.
